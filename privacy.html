privacy

No cookies. No trackers. No account.

No cookies, no analytics, no trackers, no account. What is left is what any website needs to be delivered, and that is handled by GitHub, which hosts the site.

Who

Who is responsible

PixelCake and this site are made by Phil-pje, the data controller of what this page describes. For anything about it, write to pixelcake.dev@gmail.com.

The site

pixelcake.dev

  • No cookies, no analytics, no trackers, no advertising.
  • No requests to other sites: the fonts, the pictures and the script come from pixelcake.dev itself.
  • Your browser keeps two choices of yours in its local storage (localStorage): the theme, and the language chosen with the switch, once you choose them. They stay on your device and are never sent anywhere. Clearing the data of this site in your browser removes them.

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. To deliver the pages and keep them safe, GitHub receives your IP address and the usual data of a request (the page asked for, the time, your browser), and logs the IP address of every visitor for security purposes. GitHub is a company of the United States, and processes these data there too. For transfers out of the European Union, GitHub states that it generally relies on the standard contractual clauses published by the European Commission, and that it also complies with the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, to which it has certified. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement and what GitHub says of GitHub Pages.

The app

app.pixelcake.dev

  • It runs entirely in your browser: there is no account, and no server of PixelCake behind it.
  • Your drawings and your preferences stay in your browser (in its local storage) and in the files you save on your disk.
  • Nothing you draw is sent to a server.
  • It is hosted the same way, by GitHub Pages: what is said above of the site holds for the app too.
  • Its documentation, at app.pixelcake.dev/docs/, works like the site: it keeps in your browser the theme and the language you choose, and asks no other site, its search included.
Email

If you write

If you write to pixelcake.dev@gmail.com, your address and your message are used only to reply to you, and are shared with no one. The mailbox is a Gmail account, so the messages are also handled by Google, under its privacy policy.

The legal basis is the legitimate interest in answering whoever writes (article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR). Messages are kept as long as it takes to answer, and for 24 months at most; then they are deleted.

Your rights

What you can ask

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you can ask to see the personal data about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to limit or object to its use, and to receive it. Here, in practice, that means the emails you sent: write to the address above. For the data of your visits, see GitHub's privacy statement.

You can also complain to a data protection authority: in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.
